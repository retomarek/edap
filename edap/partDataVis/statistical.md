# Statistical Plots

Statistical visualizations help to understand the distribution of values, identify outliers, and explore relationships between variables.

## Recipes

- **Boxplot** — Compare distributions across flats or months
- **Histogram** — Show how often each value occurs
- **Density Plot by Season** — Compare the distribution of a variable across the seasons
- **Correlation & Scatter** — Find variables that move together
- **Outlier Detection** — Flag implausible values with the IQR method
- **Autocorrelation** — Find repeating cycles (daily, weekly) in a time series
- **Cross-Correlation** — Find the time lag between two signals
